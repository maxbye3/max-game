There was a man named Arthur who lived in a small village that was near to a mountain.
Arthur had a dog named Winston, who was very large and possessed an amount of hair that was not normal.
One morning Arthur woke up and discovered that Winston was not existing inside the house.
He searched the garden, the shed, the kitchen and also the underneath of the bed.
This was not a suitable place for Winston because his size was too much for it.
On the kitchen table there was a note saying, "I have gone to the mountain."
Arthur felt surprise because Winston had never previously shown an interest towards mountains.
However, he decided to follow because there was not much other activity available.
After walking for many hours, Arthur met a farmer who was repairing a fence.
Arthur asked him if he had seen a large dog containing much hair.
The farmer said yes and explained that Winston had passed earlier in the morning.
He also explained that the dog was travelling with a man who wore a dark suit.
Arthur gave thanks and continued towards the mountain by his own body.
When the afternoon arrived, he reached a small town which was located on the other side.
A meeting was happening inside the town hall, so Arthur entered for finding his dog.
The mayor was sitting at a large wooden table with some people of important appearance.
Arthur explained the situation concerning Winston.
The mayor changed to a serious face and requested Arthur to sit.
He explained that Winston had arrived during the previous evening.
It appeared that the dog had become connected with an important matter of the town.
Arthur asked what was the type of this matter.
The mayor said it included a painting which was missing and a large quantity of money.
It also included an accountant who was producing much nervousness.
Arthur could not understand how Winston had entered into these matters.
The mayor said that nobody understood it either, and therefore the situation was difficult.
After some time, Winston was discovered sleeping inside a storage room.
The missing painting was also present there and was leaning against the wall.
The mayor looked at Winston and asked what had happened.
"Bark" said Winston.
Everyone immediately understood.
Arthur gave several apologies and explained that Winston was not normally a criminal.
The mayor said this was useful information for receiving.
Then he asked if Winston could remain in the town for some days.
There were many people who apparently wished to ask questions to the dog.
Arthur agreed, although Winston did not show the intention of answering.
During the next week, Winston became strangely famous across the town.
People brought food for him and requested to make photographs with his person.
A newspaper called him the most mysterious dog existing in the country.
Arthur thought this description was more than was required.
Eventually, the money which was missing was found inside the accountant's office.
The accountant admitted he had placed it there because he was afraid of losing his work.
The mayor thanked Arthur and presented Winston with a small medal.
Winston looked directly at the medal and one tear fell from his eye to the ground.
Arthur did not know the meaning of this, but felt it was better to not ask.
The mayor nodded with approval, and then he stopped doing the nodding.
Arthur and Winston returned to their home.
For many years the people continued asking Arthur about the strange events.
Arthur always said it had been a week with a very high amount of confusion.
He also said Winston had never acted completely like a normal dog.
Which was understandable, because Winston was actually a Labrador.
